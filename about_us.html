ABOUT US

CV Java Indo Nusantara

CV. JAVA INDO NUSANTARA is a manufacturer and supplier of premium coconut shell charcoal briquettes. Established in 2025, the company is committed to delivering high-quality products tailored to meet customer specifications in terms of size, shape, and packaging.

We offer flexible solutions, including private label production, custom branding, and packaging services to support our clients’ business needs. All of our briquettes are made from 100% natural coconut shell, making them an environmentally friendly and sustainable energy source.

Our products are exported to various international markets, including European countries such as Russia, Germany, France, and Turkey, as well as Middle Eastern countries including Lebanon, the UAE, and Saudi Arabia. With a production capacity of 8–10 containers per month, we ensure consistent supply and reliable delivery to our global partners.
