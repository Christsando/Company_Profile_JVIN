FROM COCONUT TO BRIQUETTES

Coconut shell charcoal briquettes are first processed into powder. The powder is then mixed with food-grade starch and water, and blended using a mixing machine until it reaches a consistent texture. Once properly blended, the mixture is transferred to a molding machine, where it is shaped and cut according to the customer’s requested size and form. After molding, the briquettes are placed on trays before being put into the oven. The drying process takes approximately 30 hours to remove moisture from the briquettes. After being taken out of the oven and cooled for 3–4 hours, the charcoal briquettes are ready for packaging. They are packed into inner plastic bags, then placed into inner boxes and finally into master cartons. The products are then ready to be shipped to their destination countries.
